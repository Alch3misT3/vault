    Connect a Browser

    Fill logins in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox. Passwords stay in Vault; the browser asks for one only when you fill.

    1. Add the Vault extension Open the browser’s Extensions page, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the folder Vault opens.
    2. Pair it Click the Vault icon in the browser’s toolbar and paste this code. It works once, for 10 minutes.

    Unlock With a Passkey

    Unlock Vault with your phone (scan a QR code; Bluetooth must be on), your Windows Hello PIN or a USB security key instead of typing the master password. The master password still works, and you need it to add a passkey.
